CASE STUDY · PERTEMPS

Making candidate progress visible

I redesigned tools that recruiters use to take a candidate from their first application through matching and placement.

The brief

At Pertemps, I led design on recruitment services across the website, branches, phone and internal operations. Once a CV entered the CRM, it was hard to see where a candidate had got to and what needed to happen next.

What I changed

I mapped the candidate journey across channels and designed the recruiter CRM and candidate management portal around the work recruiters had to do: intake, review, matching and placement. I worked on CV data extraction and created a job-title taxonomy so equivalent roles could be matched consistently.

  1. Intake
  2. CV review
  3. Matching
  4. Placement tracking

What the work produced

The tools made candidate status and progress part of the recruiting workflow. I also built a Power BI measurement board for the CRM and coordinated design handover with development teams in the UK and India.

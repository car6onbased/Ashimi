CASE STUDY · DEPARTMENT FOR EDUCATION

Designing across the apprenticeship service

An apprenticeship involves decisions by learners, employers and training providers. I mapped how those decisions connect and where a digital service needs to account for work done elsewhere.

The brief

I worked on the Department for Education apprenticeship service as a service design consultant at Methods Digital. The work followed Government Digital Service principles and design guidelines.

What I did

I ran discovery and stakeholder workshops and mapped the journey end to end. I traced the roles of apprentices, employers, training providers, colleges and awarding bodies, including how certification fits into the service.

What the map made visible

Each organisation sees a different part of the journey. An ecosystem map lets a delivery team trace who holds the information, who acts on it and where a learner depends on a handover between organisations.
